# Business Glossary
- Order Status: Standard values are 'Shipped', 'Pending', 'Delivered', 'Cancelled'.
- "أحدث الطلبات" or "latest orders": Means ORDER BY o.OrderDate DESC.
- "أعلى المبيعات" or "top sales": Means ORDER BY TotalAmount DESC.
- "الفئات" or "categories": Refers to p.Category in products table.
